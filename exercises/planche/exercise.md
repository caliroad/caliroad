## Execution

Supporting your body on pronated/neutral hands with straight arms at shoulder width, keep your body in a straight, prone position, while maintaining shoulder depression and protraction.

## Namesake

From French ‘planche’ meaning ‘plank’.

## History

The first Photograph of a full planche was from 1908 and depicted italian gymnast, Alberto Braglia performing the static hold on pommel horse.
